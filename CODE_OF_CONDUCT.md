# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want ContribRadar to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free open-source project for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different viewpoints.
- Give helpful feedback.
- Focus on improving the project.
- Respect other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling or abusive behavior.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers privately through the repository's available contact or security channels.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove inappropriate comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a respectful community.

Thank you for helping keep ContribRadar welcoming and constructive.
